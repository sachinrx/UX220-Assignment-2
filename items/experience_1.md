## Driving to Florida

### What did I do?

I drove to Florida for a trip. It took some planning and a lot of time on the road, so I had to stay focused, take breaks and be patient instead of just thinking about getting there.

### When did I do it?

I did this last December, in December 2025.

### What was good about it?

The trip was fun and gave me a break from my normal routine. I liked seeing different places along the way and having the freedom to stop instead of rushing through everything.

### What was my favourite memory?

My favourite memory was finally getting to Florida after the long drive. Being able to get out of the car, enjoy the warmer weather and relax made the whole drive feel worth it.

### What didn't I enjoy?

I didn't enjoy sitting in the car for so long or dealing with traffic. The drive could get tiring, especially when it felt like there was still a long way to go.

### What grade would I give it?

**B - for the most part enjoyable.** The drive was tiring, but I had fun and would do a road trip again. It also helped me practise planning ahead and staying patient.
