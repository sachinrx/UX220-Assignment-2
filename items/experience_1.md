## Driving to Florida

On December 28, 2025, I drove to West Palm Beach, Florida. I was the main driver both ways. I liked spending time with my family, and I actually enjoyed the drive because I got to see different cities along the way.

My favourite memory was stopping in Atlanta and shooting at a range. The part I didn’t enjoy was barely getting any sleep. Being the main driver meant I had to stay focused even when I was tired.

**Grade A.** Even with the lack of sleep, I had a great time.
