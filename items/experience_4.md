## Working on a Design Thinking Group Project

About a year ago, I worked on a group project for Design Thinking 1. I liked that most people did their part, and overall I thought it was a great experience. My favourite part was seeing us get A’s for the work we put in.

What I didn’t enjoy was that a few people didn’t do their share. That was frustrating when most of us were keeping up with our parts.

**Grade B.** I enjoyed the project and was happy with our grades, even though the work wasn’t always split evenly.
