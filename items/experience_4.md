## Keeping a Workout Routine

### What did I do?

I tried to keep a regular workout routine instead of only exercising whenever I felt like it. I set aside a few days each week and focused on being consistent, even when I did not feel like doing a long workout.

### When did I do it?

I worked on this during the summer of 2026.

### What was good about it?

I liked feeling more active and having something productive to do with my time. Following a routine also helped me see that small amounts of effort can add up if I keep doing them.

### What was my favourite memory?

My favourite memory was getting through a workout that had felt difficult a few weeks earlier. It was a small improvement, but it made me feel like the effort was actually paying off.

### What didn't I enjoy?

I didn't enjoy getting started on days when I felt tired or lazy. It was also easy to compare my progress with other people instead of focusing on my own routine.

### What grade would I give it?

**B - for the most part enjoyable.** I did not enjoy every workout, but I liked the progress. It taught me to keep working at something even when the results take time.
