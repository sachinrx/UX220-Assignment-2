## Selling Used Technology

### What did I do?

I spent time selling a few used technology items online. I took photos, wrote descriptions, compared prices and answered questions. I had to make the listings clear and keep track of messages instead of just posting them and forgetting about them.

### When did I do it?

I did this during the summer of 2025.

### What was good about it?

I liked learning how to explain what an item offered and what people wanted to know before buying it. It gave me some practice with sales and communicating clearly, which are both things I want to improve.

### What was my favourite memory?

My favourite memory was making a sale after improving a listing. Taking clearer photos and adding the details people kept asking about made the whole process easier.

### What didn't I enjoy?

I didn't enjoy answering the same questions repeatedly or dealing with people who stopped replying. It could take more time than I expected to organize everything.

### What grade would I give it?

**B - for the most part enjoyable.** The messages could get annoying, but I liked learning what makes people trust a listing. It showed me why clear information matters when someone is making a decision.
