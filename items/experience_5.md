## Buying a Car

A few months ago, I bought a car. I spent a while looking through listings and comparing prices and mileage. This one stood out because the mileage was low. My favourite part was when the salesperson gave me a deal. After all that searching, it felt good to get a car I liked at a price I was happy with.

What I didn’t enjoy was noticing a few more scratches after I bought it. I wish I’d caught them when I first looked at the car.

**Grade B.** I’m still happy with the deal, but finding the scratches afterward was annoying.
