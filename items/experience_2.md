## Working Full Time While in School

I’ve been working full time while going to school full time throughout 2026. I liked making money, learning a lot, and seeing some muscle gains along the way.

My favourite moment was getting a shoutout at work for doing a good job. It felt good to have someone notice the effort I was putting in. The part I didn’t enjoy was the stress of keeping up with both work and school, but I knew I had to make it work.

**Grade B.** I’m glad I did it, even though balancing both got stressful.
