## Learning Web Design

### What did I do?

I worked on learning HTML and CSS through my web design assignments. I practised making pages, adding links and images, and changing how things looked. I had to keep checking my work because small mistakes could affect the whole page.

### When did I do it?

I worked on this during September and October 2026.

### What was good about it?

I liked being able to see the result of something I made. Changing a few lines and seeing the page improve made it easier to stay interested. It also connected to my goal of working in UX/UI or product design.

### What was my favourite memory?

My favourite memory was getting a page to look the way I wanted after trying a few different changes. It felt good when the images, headings and spacing finally worked together.

### What didn't I enjoy?

I didn't enjoy spending a lot of time looking for one small mistake. Sometimes I would think I fixed something and then notice another part of the page needed work.

### What grade would I give it?

**B - for the most part enjoyable.** Some parts were frustrating, but I learned useful skills and got more comfortable fixing problems myself.
